1.

ERP provides stock.

2.

Warehouse Worker notices risk.

3.

Warehouse Worker creates Inventory Risk Record.

4.

Product Manager sees Inventory Risk Record inside an Actions Dashboard.

5.

Product Manager decides manually:

* discount
* promotion

* bundles
* flash sales
* etc.

6.

System uses existing Promotions functionality.

7.

Risk Record status lifecycle:
Open
→ Action Taken
→ Sold / Expired / Dismissed
